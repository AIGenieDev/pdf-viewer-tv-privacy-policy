Privacy Policy

PDF Viewer & Reader for TV (package: com.silicongenie.pdfviewer)
Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PDF Viewer & Reader for TV app (the "App") handles information. The App is published by AIGenieDev ("we", "us"). We built the App to work on your device: we do not operate any server that receives your documents or personal data, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • Your PDFs are opened and rendered locally on your device and are never uploaded to us.
  • Reading progress, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics, or the contents of your documents. We have no access to the files you open with the App.

3. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following data locally in its private app storage. This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data:

  • Library and reading data: the list of documents and folders you added, last-read page, view mode, rotation, bookmarks, and generated page thumbnails (cache).
  • Settings: theme, sort order, auto-play interval, and similar preferences.
  • FTP profiles (optional): if you choose to save an FTP server, its host, port, user name, password and path are stored locally on your device so you can reconnect. They are used only to connect to the server you entered.
  • Imported PDFs: files you import (via Wi-Fi transfer, web link or FTP) are saved to the App's own storage folder on your device.

4. Permissions and why the App needs them

  • Storage / "All files access" (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): used only to let you browse internal storage or a USB drive and open PDF files or add a PDF folder to your library, on TVs that have no system file picker. The App reads only the PDF files and folders you choose and does not upload them or access other files for any other purpose. You can revoke this permission at any time in system settings.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used only for the optional features described in Section 5.

5. Network features (only when you use them)

  • Wi-Fi Transfer: the App starts a temporary web server on your local network so you can send PDFs from your phone or computer. It is reachable only by devices on the same local network, runs only while the transfer screen is open, and no data is sent to us or to the internet.
  • Web Import: when you enter a link, the App downloads that PDF directly from the address you provided. The website you contact may receive your IP address and request details according to its own privacy policy.
  • FTP Import: when you enter an FTP server, the App connects directly to that server using the credentials you provide. Standard FTP is not encrypted; do not use passwords you rely on elsewhere. Data goes only between your device and that server.

We do not receive, log or proxy any of this traffic.

6. Third-party services

The App contains no advertising networks, analytics or tracking services. It uses open-source libraries (such as Apache Commons Net and ZXing) that run entirely on your device. Android's built-in PDF renderer is used to display documents.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone, because we do not collect it.

8. Data retention and deletion

All App data lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by removing documents or bookmarks inside the App, using "Clear cache" in Settings, clearing the App's data in Android settings, or uninstalling the App. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

9. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). It does not contain ads or social features.

10. Security

App data is stored in the Android app-private sandbox. Imported PDFs and any saved FTP credentials remain on your device; please protect access to your device. Network features use the protocols you choose (for example HTTPS for web links when the address is https, plain FTP for FTP).

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Since we do not collect or store personal data about you, these requests have no data to act on; you control all App data directly on your device as described above.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date updated above. Material changes will also be reflected in the App's Google Play listing.

13. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: support@silicon-genie.com
